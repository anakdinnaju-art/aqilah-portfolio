Hi, I'm Aqilah

I'm a graphic designer based in Melaka with 3–4 years of experience, working across branding, print, UI design, photography, videography and 3D. I enjoy exploring different sides of design instead of sticking to just one.

Most of my work is created using Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Premiere Pro and Figma, alongside SketchUp and Blender for 3D and interior visualization.

I'm naturally curious and always excited to learn something new. For me, every project is a chance to experiment, improve and turn an idea into something people can see, use and experience.
